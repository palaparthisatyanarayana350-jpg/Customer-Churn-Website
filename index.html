Key Observations

  • Month-to-month customers showed a higher observed churn rate than customers with longer contracts.
  • Churned customers had a lower average tenure.
  • Churned customers had higher average monthly charges.
  • Electronic check customers showed the highest observed churn rate among payment methods.
